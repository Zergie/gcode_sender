SAFETY BOUNDARY

Firmware is the authority

Desktop Stop is best effort. Disconnect intentionally closes the link without issuing a heater-off command. This application is not a safety controller and relies exclusively on firmware watchdogs and protections.

Always supervise live heating.What happens after disconnect is defined by firmware, not this app.
